Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · Contact: samigovera1@gmail.com

LoLTrainee (“the App”) is a League of Legends coaching companion distributed via Overwolf. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information we process

  • Riot ID / PUUID: you enter your Riot ID (GameName#TAG). The App uses Riot Games APIs to resolve your account and fetch your own match history.
  • Match and coaching data: recent matches, champion pool stats, session goals, and settings derived from that history.
  • App settings: language, role priorities, overlay preferences, and similar configuration.

2. Where data is stored

Coaching data and settings are stored locally on your device (Overwolf / browser local storage). LoLTrainee does not operate a separate cloud account backend for storing your match history.

3. Third-party services

  • Riot Games API: used to retrieve your account and match data according to Riot’s terms and your requests inside the App.
  • Overwolf: the App runs inside the Overwolf client. Overwolf may process technical data as described in Overwolf’s own privacy policy.
  • Ads / monetization (future): if Overwolf Ads are enabled, Overwolf’s advertising partners may process data under Overwolf’s monetization and privacy terms. Ads are intended for desktop windows between sessions, not invasive in-fight overlays.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not request your Riot account password.
  • We do not provide cheats, bots, automation, or fog-of-war abuse tools.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or remove Overwolf data for LoLTrainee. Uninstalling removes local App data subject to Overwolf’s uninstall behavior.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for users who meet the age requirements of League of Legends / Riot Games and Overwolf in their region.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact
