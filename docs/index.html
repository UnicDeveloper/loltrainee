LoLTrainee

Overwolf coaching companion for League of Legends — personal pool, draft focus, session goals, and analytics. Learn better. No cheats.

Get on Overwolf Privacy Policy

Built to coach, not to carry

Everything stays on your machine. Insights come from your own match history via Riot’s APIs.

  • Role priorities Pick 2–3 lanes and keep coaching focused where you actually climb.
  • Personal champion pool Pool and heat maps built from your recent games — win rate, KDA, CS/min.
  • Draft + session coaching Champ select callouts and between-game focus so the next match has a clear goal.
  • In-game overlay Light focus panel. Toggle anytime. Never automation, never fog abuse.
